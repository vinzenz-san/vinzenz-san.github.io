Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-11

This policy covers the static pages at vinzenz-dev.de and its project pages (StartGrid, PoE Trade Declutter, DefaultSorter for Amazon, Gmail Notification) — plain informational pages with no login and no user accounts. It does not cover vinzenz-dev.de/admin, a private, access-restricted area, or the browser extensions themselves, each of which has its own Privacy Policy linked from its own project page covering what that extension accesses.

No cookies, no tracking

These pages set no cookies, run no analytics, and load no third-party trackers or fonts. Nothing you do on these pages is tracked or profiled.

Hosting & server logs

These pages are static files served by GitHub Pages (GitHub/Microsoft) via Cloudflare as DNS and CDN in front of the domain. Both providers process your IP address automatically, as part of routing and serving every request — this happens at the infrastructure level, not through anything built into these pages, and neither I nor these pages store or see that data. Retention is controlled by GitHub and Cloudflare, not by me; see their own privacy policies for details:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Legal basis

Processing IP addresses for the technical delivery of the pages is based on a legitimate interest in operating a functioning, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the usual GDPR rights — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability — plus the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For anything within my control, contact me below. For the server-log data GitHub and Cloudflare process as infrastructure providers, their own privacy policies explain how to reach them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
